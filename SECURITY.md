##SECURITY POLICY | "WHOLESALER-PRO" VIRTUAL EDITION

Overview
Home‑Link Realty Group is committed to maintaining a secure environment for our users and partners. This document provides a high‑level overview of our security practices. It is intentionally limited in scope and does not create contractual obligations, warranties, or service guarantees.

For contractual security commitments, please refer to your service agreement.

Reporting a Vulnerability
If you believe you’ve discovered a security issue, please contact:

security@gotcashoffer.com

We will review your report and respond in a reasonable timeframe.

Please avoid publicly disclosing potential vulnerabilities until we have completed our investigation.

General Security Practices
We follow widely recognized industry practices to help safeguard our platform, including:

Access controls aligned with role responsibilities

Multi‑factor authentication for administrative access

Encryption of data in transit using modern TLS configurations

Regular updates to dependencies and platform components

Continuous monitoring for abnormal or unauthorized activity

Internal review processes for code changes and deployments

These practices evolve as our platform and threat landscape change.

Responsible Disclosure Guidelines
We ask that security researchers:

Act in good faith and avoid accessing data that does not belong to them

Avoid actions that degrade service availability or performance

Provide sufficient detail for us to reproduce the issue

Allow reasonable time for remediation before public disclosure

We do not authorize testing that violates applicable laws.

Third‑Party Services
We rely on reputable third‑party providers for infrastructure, authentication, and communications. These providers maintain their own independent security certifications (such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001).

We do not publish internal configurations, vendor contracts, or architectural details.

Limitations
To avoid misunderstandings, this document does not include:

Uptime guarantees

Recovery time objectives (RTO/RPO)

Detailed architecture diagrams

Specific encryption algorithms or key‑management procedures

Commitments to particular operational controls

Legal warranties or representations

These topics are addressed only in private contractual agreements where appropriate.

Updates
This document may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our security posture or platform capabilities.
