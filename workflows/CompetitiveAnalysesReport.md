\#\# PRIORITY GAPS TO ADDRESS

\#\#\# Tier 1 \(Critical \- Revenue Impact\)

1\. \*\*Home Value Estimator Tool\*\* \- Every competitor has this; customers expect it

2\. \*\*Video Testimonials\*\* \- Emotional proof \(HomeVestors, Opendoor use heavily\)

3\. \*\*Incentive/Promotion\*\* \- Offers $2K bonus or similar \(OfferPad does this\)

4\. \*\*Interactive Process Visual\*\* \- Step\-by\-step visual for clarity

\#\#\# Tier 2 \(Important \- Conversion Optimization\)

5\. \*\*More robust FAQ section\*\* \- Address common objections

6\. \*\*Resource library\*\* \- 10\-20 SEO\-optimized articles minimum

7\. \*\*Chat/support widget\*\* \- Live or AI\-powered

8\. \*\*Mobile optimization audit\*\* \- Ensure parity with competitors

\#\#\# Tier 3 \(Nice to Have \- Brand Building\)

9\. \*\*Publicly disclosed satisfaction rating\*\* \- If you have 95%\+ data

10\. \*\*Free local move offer\*\* \- If feasible \(OfferPad's killer feature\)

11\. \*\*Flexible closing timeline\*\* \(8\-60 days\) \- Competitor differentiator
